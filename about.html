About Campus Market

What we do

Campus Market helps students buy, sell, and exchange items within a trusted campus community. Every listing is tied to real accounts, verified sellers, and safe meetup options.

Why it matters
  • Lower-cost access to everyday essentials.
  • Faster local transactions without middlemen.
  • Safer meetups using campus-approved zones.
Need help?

Contact the admin team through the support channel in the app or email the platform administrator for account or listing support.
